About

I am a senior software engineer in Lahore, Pakistan, and I build full-stack web applications and the LLM systems inside them. At Vroozi, a procure-to-pay platform, I designed and implemented a document-extraction pipeline on Gemini and a catalog search service with an LLM re-ranker, and built a LangGraph purchasing agent. All three are in production; in our team of four, each of us works across the front end, the backend and the LLM pipelines. I like to settle a design in discussion first, then pair on the hard parts. From 2021 to 2023 I was at Rolustech, building Angular and Node.js applications such as Panavid's warehouse portal.

Where and how I work

I work from Lahore, Pakistan (UTC+5, no daylight saving). I can cover full European or full US business hours, depending on the team.

I can work as a remote employee, on a direct contract or through an employer of record, and I would relocate for the right role.
